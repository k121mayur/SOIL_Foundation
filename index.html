Welcome to the SOIL Foundation

SOIL (Supporting Optimal Improvement of Lives) an ambitious initiative led by young development professionals, aims to demonstrate the theory of giving back to society. The Western Ghats, known for their significant contribution to ecological balance, face challenges due to deforestation, mining, encroachment, unscientific forest product collection, and inadequate maintenance of water bodies. The founders of SOIL, hailing from this region, recognize the impending threats to all living beings and are actively addressing them through their organization. Their focus is on sustainable practices and community development. Read more..
